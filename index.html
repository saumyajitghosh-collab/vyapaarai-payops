How it works

Connect what you already use

Your bank statement, payment app and gateway reports, and bills from Tally, Zoho or Excel. No new QR code, no change for your customers.

Every payment is matched

Rules you can read: payer UPI ID, exact amount, bill number in the remark, oldest bill first for part-payments. Anything unclear becomes an exception, never a guess.

Act from WhatsApp

Approve matches, send reminders with a pay link, and ask what came in, who owes you, and what fees cost this month.
